What if resistance was never the problem?

What if instead of building ever more sophisticated strategies to overcome resistance, we could dissolve the conditions that create it — so resistance never forms in the first place? Not by persuading people or training them or pressuring them. By addressing the upstream conditions — perspective, energy, capacity, habit — that determine whether a human system resists or adapts.

The Analogy

Before 1846,
surgery was torture.

Surgeons operated on patients who were fully conscious. Patients screamed. They thrashed. Some chose death over a follow-up procedure. The resistance was total — and entirely rational.

Then, on October 16, 1846, ether was administered before a surgical procedure at Massachusetts General Hospital. The patient lost consciousness. The surgeon operated in silence. Everything changed that day.

But the surgery did not change. The incisions were the same. The instruments were the same. What changed was the patient's relationship to the procedure. Anesthesia did not make surgery better. It made surgery survivable.

Change Anesthesiology applies this same principle to organisations: you never fight resistance. You dissolve the conditions that create it — so change happens without trauma.

The war is over. Not because it was won.
Because it was the wrong war.

— Change Anesthesiology

The Model

Four zones. One causal sequence.
One empty zone that changes everything.

Not a cycle. Not a matrix. A sequence — one zone feeding the next, each creating the conditions that make the next one possible. Skip a zone, and the model does not work.

Zone 01

Broaden Perspectives

A person with narrow perspectives sees change from one angle: their own. From that single vantage point, change looks like loss. Broaden the perspective, and the unfamiliar becomes navigable — not because it changed, but because the person can now see it differently.

Zone 02

Expand Comfort Zone

Seeing differently is not enough if the system is depleted. OmniMeta Mindfulness maintains cognitive coherence. Tinergy manages the energy resource. Together they shift comfort from static external anchors to dynamic internal ones that travel with the person through transition.

Zone 03

Reduce Resistance

No tool. No workshop. No framework. This zone has no intervention. Resistance dissolves when the upstream conditions are addressed. The empty zone is not a gap in the methodology. It is the methodology.

The result — not the target

Zone 04

Increase Pace of Change

Acceleration without stabilisation is a spike, not a shift. The Habit Machine locks new behaviour into daily identity through visible, tangible, repeated action. Each coin dropped is not a task completed. It is a vote for a new identity.

Broaden → Expand → Reduce → Increase

The Methodology

Morphosis: Five Dimensions

Five capabilities that, when practiced daily, expand the human system's capacity to absorb continuous change without resistance. Not workshops that happen once and fade. Embedded, daily, self-administered practices.

Zone 1 · Broaden

White Space Thinking

Chapter 6 — The Pause That Makes Everything Possible

Creates the cognitive gap — the pause where reflection becomes possible. Without the gap, there is no room for a new perspective to enter. Modern work has not just eliminated these gaps. It has structurally damaged the brain's ability to think.

Zone 1 · Broaden

Perspective Thinking

Chapter 7 — Seeing What You Haven't Seen

Fills the gap with new ways of seeing — through illustrated insights, books, card decks, conversation starters, and video subscriptions embedded in the environment. Passive perspective exposure at organisational scale.

Zone 2 · Expand

OmniMeta Mindfulness

Chapter 8 — Expanding What You Can Handle

Maintains cognitive coherence under complexity through the BOSE Framework (Body, Observations, Senses, Energy) and the MetaCycle — a structured rhythm for transitioning between demands with intention rather than reaction.

Zone 2 · Expand

Tinergy & Energy

Chapter 9 — The Resource Nobody Manages

Tinergy = Time × Energy. Time is fixed; energy is the variable. The system tracks energy across five dimensions — physical, mental, emotional, spiritual, social — and provides a daily diagnostic for individuals and teams.

Zone 4 · Increase

The Habit Machine

Chapter 10 — The Lock That Holds the Door Open

A physical, screen-free, 3D-printed daily habit tracker. Each day, the user drops a coin to mark the completion of a specific behaviour. Made in Canada. Because 45% of daily behaviour is habitual — and habits determine whether gains persist or decay.
